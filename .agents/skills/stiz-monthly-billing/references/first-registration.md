# First student registration: complete workflow

Use this when registering a genuinely new student or repairing a registration that stopped after creating the student row. The website is the operating source of truth; Rallyz is still the live parent-invitation and invoice channel. Do not create an October 2026 or later Sheet ledger unless the user resumes it. Read an older Sheet period only when reconciling that period.

1. Resolve identity before mutation. Compare the site application and student ID, guardian contact, birth date or school where available, class, effective month, and Toss memo. A misspelled Toss name remains HELD until the user or another reliable source links it to the stable student ID. Check that the site and Rallyz do not already have the student; avoid duplicate registration.
2. Capture the **actual first instruction date** separately from the date the student record was entered. Check the academic calendar, requested weekdays and periods, approved status, and whether the student attended or paid earlier. A retroactive first date must not be silently replaced by the registration timestamp. Record a non-editable Rallyz start-date discrepancy in the student memo and local handoff; do not remove and re-add a class merely to rewrite history.
3. Confirm website student, guardian, approved enrollments, status, shuttle request, and class/program fee. Sync the matching Rallyz branch and classes, then re-read the Rallyz student detail. A parent contact with the student's same number may have a different label in the Rallyz invitation dialog; identify the exact destination number and account before sending.
4. Split the paid first period from the next billable period. For a partial month, calculate only confirmed remaining sessions under the live fee policy and academic calendar. Check Toss approval and cancellation, existing Rallyz unpaid/paid invoices, and site Payment separately. A paid prior period must not be billed again. Keep site Payment reconciliation read-only; never auto-create or update a site Payment solely from a Toss order.
5. Check the next month's class frequency, tuition, discount, shuttle, service period, due date, and existing invoice. Prepare one invoice per supported Rallyz class row without double-applying a multi-class discount or shuttle. Do not issue zero-value, duplicate, paused or withdrawn invoices. The action preview should distinguish any paid historical invoice to be recorded from a new payable invoice and state exactly what notification each would send.
   - Read the official annual plan for the effective service month and count actual sessions for every registered weekday. The annual plan's opening and closing events may cross calendar months; for October 2026 the verified period is 2026-10-06 through 2026-11-02, with four Tuesday and four Thursday sessions. The website code calculates these dates from live annual events, so source code alone does not prove the current event dates.
   - Rallyz invoice creation first requires the **billing year/month** modal to be confirmed. On the final confirmation screen, set both service-period dates and verify their displayed values before issue. Its calendar may appear to select the prior week when clicking a date; trust only the dates displayed in the final confirmation, and do not issue if they are wrong.
6. With the user's specific authorization for invoice and invitation, use Rallyz to issue the verified payable invoice, send its requested parent notification, and invite the verified guardian once. Do not assume a click succeeded: inspect the invoice creation result, payment-list status and period, delivery record, and invitation success. If the invoice UI fails to load or any amount/recipient is unresolved, leave only that step PARTIAL and do not fabricate completion or use a different student's amount.
7. Re-read the site student/enrollments and invoice/payment state, Rallyz student/class/guardian connection, invoice and delivery state, and historical Sheet only when applicable. Record applied, skipped, held and failed steps with IDs, before/after values and the next safe retry point. Do not resend an invitation or recreate an invoice after an uncertain response until its current state has been checked.
